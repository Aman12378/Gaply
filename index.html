What's in Gaply

Skill gap analyzer

Paste any posting to see the skills it asks for, your match score, and a ranked list of gaps.

Resume reader

Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT resume and Gaply pulls out your skills and contact details.

Role comparison

Saved history

Keep past analyses and check how your match score changes as you learn.

How it works

  1. Paste a posting

    Copy text from any job board, or load a sample role.

  2. Gaply finds the skills

    Languages, tools, concepts and soft skills, marked must-have or nice-to-have.

  3. Work through your gaps

    Get your match score, a learning checklist and keywords for your CV.
